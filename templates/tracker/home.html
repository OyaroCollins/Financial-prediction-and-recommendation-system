About SmartFinance

SmartFinance is a final year project developed at the Catholic University of Eastern Africa in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the Degree in Computer Science.

Built by Collins Oyaro Ondieki, this system is designed to help small businesses in Kenya and beyond track their finances, predict cash flow, and receive intelligent recommendations — all in one simple platform.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between basic finance tracking tools and advanced, AI-powered decision-support systems, making intelligent financial management accessible to every small business owner.
